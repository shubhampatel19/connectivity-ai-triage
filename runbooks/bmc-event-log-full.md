# BMC system event log filling up

## Symptoms
The BMC warns that its system event log is 80 to 95% full and will overwrite its oldest entries. Routine on long-running devices, and harmless on its own: hardware faults still get logged.

## Fix
Clear the event log during the next maintenance visit. Not a cause of thermal, power or disk faults.
